# Kaputt! 3.0 — Open Questions

## H01 — Decision Density

How often are both Attack and Defense strategically plausible?

We want a mix of:
- obvious decisions;
- contextual decisions;
- difficult decisions.

Avoid a trivial threshold policy dominating play.

## H02 — Polar dominance

Does seeing:
- 1 make Defense too automatic?
- 6 make Attack too automatic?

Key structural feature to test:
- reveal 1 + Defense → 33.33% chance of NtB 2;
- reveal 6 + Attack → 33.33% chance of 36.

## H03 — NtB dynamics

Measure actual time spent in:
- Green / low pressure;
- Tension / mid pressure;
- Danger / high pressure.

Test whether the hypothesized cycle appears naturally:
Attack → escalation → pressure → Defense → decompression.

## H04 — Extreme balance

Is 5.56% event frequency enough to feel memorable?

Does:
- Attack Extreme = 36
- Defense Extreme = 2

produce healthy swing rather than arbitrary swing?

## H05 — Fortune Point economy

Do PF still improve the game?

If yes:
- how are they earned?
- when can they be spent?
- is cancellation of Kaputt! still desirable?
- is NtB reduction still desirable?

## H06 — Initial NtB

Determine the correct starting NtB empirically.

## H07 — Game modes

Rebuild and validate separately:
- Cooperative
- Vs
- King of the Hill
- Battle Royale

Each mode needs its own:
- victory condition;
- Kaputt threshold;
- scoring target;
- PF ownership;
- next-player rule.

## H08 — Competitive pathologies

Measure:
- first-player advantage;
- runaway leader;
- kingmaking;
- focus fire;
- stalling;
- comeback probability.

## H09 — Cooperative pathologies

Measure:
- alpha-player dominance;
- trivial consensus;
- whether leaving a high NtB creates meaningful team tension.

## H10 — Duration

Track:
- turns per game;
- real minutes;
- score velocity;
- Kaputt frequency;
- Extreme frequency.
