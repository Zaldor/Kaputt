# Kaputt! 3.0 — Decision Log

## D001 — Remove special rules from all doubles

**Status:** Accepted for K3-E1

All doubles resolve normally.

Reason:
- restores the full mathematical 2d6 space;
- reduces rule overhead;
- stops frequent special events from overriding the core decision.

## D002 — Extreme occurs only on 1–6 / 6–1

**Status:** Accepted for K3-E1

Frequency:
- old any-double special: 6/36 = 16.67%;
- Extreme: 2/36 = 5.56%.

Reason:
- makes the event rarer and more memorable;
- gives 1 and 6 semantic meaning;
- preserves more normal resolutions.

## D003 — Extreme polarizes the declared action

**Status:** Accepted for K3-E1

- Attack Extreme: 1 becomes 6 → 36.
- Defense Extreme: 6 becomes 1 → 2, scoring 1.

Reason:
- special event preserves player agency;
- Attack remains escalation;
- Defense remains de-escalation.

## D004 — Keep 1–1 and 6–6 natural

**Status:** Accepted for K3-E1

No additional rule for minimum or maximum doubles.

Reason:
The combination with Extreme creates deliberate symmetry:

- Defense reaches 2 via 1–1 or Extreme.
- Attack reaches 36 via 6–6 or Extreme.

Global probability of each extreme result:
3/36 = 8.33%.

Conditional probability:
- reveal 1 + choose Defense → 2 with second die 1 or 6 = 33.33%;
- reveal 6 + choose Attack → 36 with second die 1 or 6 = 33.33%.

This symmetry is considered a feature, not a defect.

## D005 — Do not redesign NtB yet

**Status:** Protected pending simulation

Current hypothesis:
Attack escalates NtB and Defense decompresses it, creating an endogenous pressure cycle.

Do not add resets or caps until dynamic simulations show a concrete problem.
