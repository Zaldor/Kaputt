HOW TO PLAY

  1. Roll both dice. Both results stay hidden.
  2. Tap either die. You decide which one to reveal first.
  3. Choose Attack or Defense. Your choice is locked before the second reveal.
  4. Reveal the other die. See your result, then pass the turn.

ATTACK ×

Multiply the dice. The result must be strictly greater than the Number to Beat. Score the result and set it as the new target. Otherwise, score nothing and take one Kaputt; the target stays put.

DEFENSE +

Score the higher die, set the target to the sum, and take no Kaputt.

THE EXTREME: 1 & 6

Attack becomes 36. Defense sets the target to 2 and scores 1. An Attack of 36 still fails against a target of 36. Doubles are normal rolls.

THE WIN

Reach the score target, or let your opponent reach the Kaputt limit. The default is first to 100; five Kaputts and out.
