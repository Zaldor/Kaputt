# Kaputt! 3.0 — Playtest Protocol

## Record for every session

- build version;
- mode;
- player count;
- player familiarity;
- total duration;
- total turns;
- final score;
- Kaputt count;
- Extreme count;
- PF earned/spent if enabled.

## Decision observations

For selected turns record:

- NtB before turn;
- first die revealed;
- Attack or Defense;
- confidence in decision;
- whether the player felt the choice was obvious;
- result;
- NtB after turn.

## Post-game questions

1. Did the first reveal create tension?
2. How often did the choice feel obvious?
3. Did Attack feel meaningfully risky?
4. Did Defense feel like an active choice rather than a fallback?
5. Was the Estremo memorable?
6. Did any rule feel arbitrary?
7. Did the game overstay its welcome?
8. Would you immediately play again?

## Important

Do not explain design hypotheses to blind playtesters before the session.
